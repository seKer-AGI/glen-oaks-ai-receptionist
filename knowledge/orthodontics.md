# Orthodontics and Invisalign

## Orthodontics Overview
Glen Oaks Dental Professionals offers orthodontics for children and adults, including clear ceramic braces, traditional metal braces, and Invisalign clear aligners. Services also include treatment with orthognathic surgery, limited treatment, and retention. Dr. Catherine Woo and Dr. Mariah Pewarski are the orthodontists.

## Children's Orthodontics
The website recommends a first orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Phase I interceptive treatment begins before all permanent teeth emerge.

## Adult Orthodontics
The website notes about 20 percent of orthodontic patients are over 21, and that success is independent of age. Modern appliances are less noticeable than older ones.

## Invisalign
Yes, Invisalign is available. Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners made of medical grade plastic to straighten teeth without metal brackets or wires. A 3-D computer plan is created, and each aligner is worn about two weeks. Aligners are removable for eating, drinking and cleaning. Invisalign Express is for mild problems like minor crowding and spacing, usually under six months. Whether a person is a candidate is decided by the orthodontist.
