# Sedation

## Sedation Options
Yes, sedation is available. Options include pills, nitrous oxide, and IV sedation. The practice describes its approach as painless dentistry with multiple sedation options.

## IV Sedation Dentistry
IV sedation is given through the bloodstream so the level can be controlled. Patients stay conscious and responsive, and most remember little afterward. Vital signs are monitored throughout. Patients need a driver afterward. It is provided by Dr. Kavi Shah and certified dentists at the practice. The website says it may suit patients with dental anxiety, multiple procedures, or a sensitive gag reflex. Whether it suits a specific patient is decided by the dentist.
