# Patient Education and Technology

## Patient Education
The website has a patient education section covering brushing and flossing technique, choosing electric or manual toothbrushes, bad breath, nutrition and dental health, and cavity prevention with fluoride. There is also a dental insights section.

## Technology
The practice uses digital x-rays with minimal radiation, intraoral cameras, 3D CT scans, and digital scanning systems. Invisalign and Teeth In A Day use 3D imaging and scanning.

## Smile Gallery
The website includes a smile gallery and a staff directory.
