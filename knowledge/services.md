# Services

## Services Overview
Glen Oaks Dental Professionals offers dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, Invisalign, veneers and laminates, crowns and bridges, inlays and onlays, root canal treatment, tooth-colored fillings, pediatric dentistry, dental sealants, dentures, gum treatment, TMJ treatment, IV sedation dentistry, and Teeth In A Day. General dentistry and oral surgery such as wisdom teeth extraction are also available.

## General Dentistry
The practice provides general dentistry with a prevention-oriented approach, including exams, digital x-rays, and tooth-colored fillings. Dr. Haresh Shah, Dr. Gargi Gajera and Dr. Peterson Chao provide general dentistry.

## Cosmetic Dentistry
Cosmetic procedures are done in-house without referring patients to specialists. Treatments include composite bonding for chipped, broken or discolored teeth, dental veneers, inlays and onlays, teeth whitening (at home or in-office), and dental implants. The practice aims to preserve existing tooth structure.

## Veneers and Laminates
Veneers and laminates are thin porcelain or resin shells bonded to the teeth to change shape, shade and position. They need minimal to no tooth removal. A complete dental exam determines suitability.

## Crowns and Bridges
Crowns (caps) restore severely damaged teeth and range from full porcelain to full metal. Bridges replace one or more missing teeth and are bonded in place, removable only by a dentist.

## Inlays and Onlays
Inlays and onlays are long-lasting fillings for decay and structural damage.

## Tooth-Colored Fillings
Tooth-colored fillings are made of durable plastic composite that matches natural teeth. They can be done in the same appointment as dental sealants.

## Dental Sealants
Dental sealants are protective coatings, often applied to permanent molars, to help prevent decay.

## Root Canal Treatment
Root canal treatment removes infected or dead tissue from inside the tooth, then disinfects and seals it. A crown is typically placed afterward. Root canals are performed daily at the practice. Dr. Peterson Chao has endodontic expertise.

## Dentures
Three denture options: implant-retained dentures secured by 4 to 6 implants with no adhesive, standard full dentures that rely on suction, and partial dentures that attach to remaining teeth.

## Gum Treatment (Periodontics)
Periodontics focuses on gum health and the structures supporting teeth. Services include prevention, diagnosis and treatment of gum disease, plus cosmetic gum procedures like crown lengthening and gum grafting. Dr. David Heering is the periodontist.

## TMJ Treatment
The practice offers diagnostic evaluation for jaw joint problems such as pain, clicking, popping and locking. Treatment options include night guards, bite guards, and bite adjustments, based on each person's needs.

## Pediatric Dentistry
Pediatric care includes cleanings, exams, x-rays, jaw and tooth development checks, hygiene instruction, and sealants, with a focus on keeping children comfortable. The website does not state a recommended age for a first dental visit.

## Oral Surgery
The practice has an oral surgeon for wisdom teeth extraction. Dr. Kavi Shah performs extractions with bone grafting, and Dr. Gargi Gajera trained in oral surgery.
