# Practice Overview

## About Glen Oaks Dental Professionals
Glen Oaks Dental Professionals is a dental practice in Queens, New York with two offices: Glen Oaks (NY 11004) and Jamaica (NY 11432). The practice has operated for over 30 years and takes a prevention-oriented approach with a full range of dental services.

## Patient Experience
The practice describes its care as painless dentistry with multiple sedation options. Entertainment such as TV and music is provided during procedures. Digital x-rays are used with minimal radiation exposure.

## Appointment Availability
The practice offers after-work, weekend, emergency, and walk-in appointments. Patients can request an appointment through the website contact form or by calling an office.

## Specialties on Staff
The practice has a pedodontist (pediatric care), periodontist (gum treatment), implantologist (dental implants), oral surgeon (wisdom teeth extraction), orthodontist (braces and Invisalign), and general and cosmetic dentists.

## Online Presence
The practice has Facebook and Instagram accounts and a patient education section on its website.
