# Doctors and Staff

## Doctors Overview
Glen Oaks Dental Professionals has seven doctors: Dr. Kavi H. Shah, Dr. Haresh D. Shah, Dr. David Heering, Dr. Catherine Woo, Dr. Gargi Gajera, Dr. Peterson Chao, and Dr. Mariah Pewarski. Three dental hygienists also support patient care.

## Dr. Kavi H. Shah, DDS
Cosmetic and implant dentistry. Graduated from Nova Southeastern University in 2013 after Emory University. Certified in IV sedation dentistry and skilled in nitrous oxide sedation. Experienced in extractions with bone grafting, PRF treatments, implant placement and full-mouth reconstruction.

## Dr. Haresh D. Shah, DDS
Cosmetic and general dentistry. Graduated from NYU College of Dentistry in 1980. Over 32 years in private practice in Jamaica and Glen Oaks, with oral surgery internship and coursework in implants, prosthetics and veneers.

## Dr. David Heering, DMD
Periodontist and implant surgeon. Trained at Tufts and Boston University in periodontics. Staff periodontist at Hamilton Hospital with more than 10,000 implants placed.

## Dr. Catherine Woo, Orthodontist
Board certified orthodontist and Diplomate of the American Board of Orthodontics. Columbia University trained. Trained in conventional braces, Invisalign, Damon braces and TADs.

## Dr. Mariah Pewarski, DMD
Orthodontist. Harvard and University of Pennsylvania, with orthodontics residency at Albert Einstein College of Medicine and Montefiore. Trained in metal and ceramic braces, Invisalign and surgical orthodontics.

## Dr. Gargi Gajera, DDS
Implant and general dentistry. Emory and Nova Southeastern, with residency at Winthrop University Hospital. Experienced in extensive dental reconstruction.

## Dr. Peterson Chao, DDS
Endodontic and general dentistry. University at Buffalo dental school graduate. Root canal and full-mouth rehabilitation expertise. Speaks English, Mandarin and Spanish.

## Dental Hygienists
Marcela Canola, RDH; Carolina Fritman, RDH; and Sonya Caceres, RDH. They focus on preventive care, patient education and periodontal health.
