# Dental Implants

## Dental Implants
Yes, the practice provides dental implant treatment and describes itself as a leader in dental implants in the Queens area. A dental implant is a titanium cylinder that replaces the missing tooth root and can support a crown or anchor dentures. Implants are placed daily. Consultations are offered at both offices.

## Implant Candidates and Process
The website says most patients with adequate bone can have implants, with X-rays and CT scans used to assess bone. The process is placement, a healing period, then attaching components and a crown. Doctors who place implants include Dr. Kavi Shah, Dr. David Heering and Dr. Gargi Gajera.

## Teeth In A Day
Teeth In A Day is same-day full-arch implant replacement: a whole upper or lower set of teeth supported by implants and a fixed bridge, with no removable dentures. It uses 3D scanning and printing instead of traditional molds. A full arch uses a minimum of 4 implants. Single tooth, multiple teeth, and denture stabilization options are also offered.
