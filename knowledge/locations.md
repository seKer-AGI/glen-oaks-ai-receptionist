# Locations and Hours

## Office Locations
Glen Oaks Dental Professionals has two offices: Glen Oaks and Jamaica, both in Queens, New York.

## Glen Oaks Office
Address: 257-10 Union Turnpike, Glen Oaks, NY 11004. Phone: 718-343-7700. The office is on Union Turnpike across from Burlington Coat Factory, just west of LIJ Hospital. Ample parking is available.

## Jamaica Office
Address: 87-34 Parsons Blvd., Jamaica, NY 11432. Phone: 718-739-4845. The office is on Parsons Boulevard near Hillcrest High School. Ample parking is available.

## Office Hours
The website's contact page lists both offices as open Monday to Friday 10:30 AM to 6:30 PM and Saturday 9:00 AM to 3:30 PM. The website's offices page describes weekday last appointments at 6 PM and weekends 9 AM to 1 PM. Because the website lists these differently, patients should confirm exact hours with the office. After-work and weekend appointments are available.

## Scheduling
Appointments can be requested by phone or through the contact form on the website, which asks for name, email, phone, preferred location, and message.
